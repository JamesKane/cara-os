# Code of Conduct

CaraOS is a technical project with a narrow mission: a cleanroom
C23/RISC-V reimplementation of the AmigaOS Release 2 specification.
Our goal is to resurrect the architectural soul of V36+ for modern
open silicon, stripping away modern abstractions to return to a 
"user is god" model. Everything else is out of scope, and that 
includes the political fights people would otherwise bring with them.

If you're here to ship the system, you are welcome.

## What we welcome

- Respectful technical collaboration on code, design, performance,
  hardware bring-up, documentation, and tooling.
- Hard, specific feedback. Disagreement, when it's about the work,
  is the point.
- Contributors of any background, location, or identity who show up
  to make the system better.
- Patches, bug reports, benchmarks, hardware reports, and
  documentation fixes from anyone — no contributor licence
  agreement, no demographic gatekeeping, no ideological litmus test.

## What is out of scope

- **Political activism — Left, Right, or any other axis.** This
  project is not a vehicle for any political movement. Contributors
  are not expected to adopt, oppose, signal, or denounce any
  political position in order to participate. Bring it elsewhere.
- Personal attacks, harassment, sustained hostility, dogpiling,
  doxxing, or threats of any kind.
- Demands that other contributors adopt your views, sign your
  open letter, boycott a person or organisation, or take a public
  stance on anything outside the technical work of this repository.
- Off-topic flame wars in commits, pull requests, issues, chat, or
  anywhere else the project's name is attached.

The test is simple: does this exchange move CaraOS forward? If
no, it doesn't belong in the project's spaces.

## How disputes are handled

1. A maintainer will privately point the contributor at this
   document and ask them to drop the off-topic thread.
2. If it continues, the contributor is asked to step away from the
   thread, issue, or PR for a cooling-off period.
3. If it still continues, contribution access is revoked.

Maintainers' decisions are final. There is no committee, no public
trial, no appeals process. This is a small project; cycles go to
the kernel, not to mediation.

## Reporting

Concerns about conduct can be raised privately with the project
maintainers. Contact details are listed in the repository's
maintainer file or commit history. Reports are read by maintainers
only and are not made public.

## Scope

This document applies to behaviour in any space where the project's
name is attached: this repository, its issue tracker and pull
requests, project chat, mailing lists, and conferences or events
where someone is representing CaraOS.

What contributors do in their own time, under their own name, in
spaces unrelated to CaraOS is none of the project's business.
